APAC Payroll

Compliance Calendar

Every recurring statutory payroll deadline across 9 markets, in one reference.

Reference dates only, compiled from public guidance as of August 2026 — several markets have rules in transition (e.g. Australia's Payday Super phase-in, India's Income Tax Act 2025 form changes, Hong Kong's proposed MPF cap increase). "Actual payment date" adjustments use each country's major known 2026 public holidays and weekends; moon-sighting or unconfirmed lunar-calendar holidays (Eid, some Hindu/Buddhist observances) aren't all included, so a shown date could still shift. Malaysia brings the date forward (earlier) when it lands on a holiday; every other market pushes it back (later) — both per each country's own stated convention. Confirm exact figures against the local authority or your in-country counsel before relying on them operationally.
